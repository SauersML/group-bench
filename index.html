FIELD NOTES

A map, with evidence.

Each property is a yes-or-no question about a countable discrete group. Your filters form a conjunction: every requirement must hold at once.

Exists means an actual example in the catalog is known to satisfy every requirement. Click its facts to trace the evidence.

Impossible means the requirements contradict sourced mathematical rules. The atlas displays the conflicting requirements and the deductions.

Unresolved means neither certificate is available in this catalog. A consistent Boolean assignment is never treated as a group. Missing facts are never treated as false.

This is a curated starting atlas, not a complete classification. Sources are attached to definitions, example facts, and rules. Some examples link to independently checked formal proofs, identified in their provenance. The atlas’s own deductions are not Lean-checked certificates.

Growth and algorithmic properties include finite generation in their definitions. “Simple” excludes the trivial group; “free” includes it. A group can have some torsion without being a torsion group.

Each example has a first-proof date field and source, with unknown dates stated explicitly. Construction, announcement, publication, and formal verification dates are distinguished. A date is not automatically applied to properties proved later, and an earliest recorded source does not by itself certify historical priority.

The map is a two-dimensional slice of a larger property space. Choose any properties in Edit rows or Edit columns, including their “Does not have” versions. Add filters for requirements that apply to every cell. Links preserve your selections.
